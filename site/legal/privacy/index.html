Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Version: 1.0

This policy describes how OpenSight ("we", "us") handles information in connection with OpenSight — the open-source software, the hosted service at app.getopensight.org, and the website at getopensight.org.

1. The short version

  • If you run OpenSight yourself, we receive nothing. The software runs entirely on your infrastructure.
  • If you use the hosted service, we process your workspace's data to run it for you. We do not sell it, advertise against it, or use its content to build data products.
  • Our product analytics record counts and flags at the workspace level — never the content of your data, questions, or dashboards.

2. Self-hosted OpenSight

The open-source software does not send us your data. It has an optional product-analytics feature that is off by default. If an operator turns it on, it sends usage counts and feature flags (for example, that a data source was connected or a dashboard was saved), identified at the installation level, not the person level. It never sends dataset contents, query text, results, or dashboard contents. Self-hosted operators are responsible for the data in their own installations.

3. Information we collect on the hosted service

Account information. Names and email addresses of workspace owners and members, workspace names, and role assignments — provided at provisioning, signup, or invitation.

Workspace content. The data you connect, upload, or create: data source configurations, datasets, analyses, dashboards, scheduled reports, and uploaded files. We process this content to provide the service. Where you connect your own databases or warehouses, queries run against them under your credentials and the results are displayed to your members; we do not copy source data beyond what rendering, caching, and the features you use require.

Usage and metering records. Counts of metered activity (API calls, queries run, storage used, AI questions, emails sent, data egress) used to enforce plan quotas and bill correctly.

Payment information. Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We receive the subscription status, plan, and billing identifiers from Stripe. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers.

Technical logs. Server and security logs (timestamps, IP addresses, request paths, error records) kept for reliability, security, and abuse prevention.

Communications. Messages you send us — support email, access requests, and feedback submitted through the product's Ideas feature (Ideas submissions are visible only to the submitter and to us).

4. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the service: provisioning workspaces, running queries, rendering dashboards, delivering scheduled reports and alerts you configure.
  • To meter usage, enforce quotas, and bill subscriptions.
  • To secure the service: authentication, audit logging, detecting abuse such as trial or email-feature misuse.
  • To improve the product using aggregate usage counts (section 2's rules apply: counts and flags, workspace-level, no content).
  • To communicate with workspace owners about the service, billing, and material changes to our terms or this policy.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use workspace content for advertising, profiling of individuals, or training AI models.

5. AI features

Ask O's generative mode is optional. In managed mode, your question and the schema context needed to answer it (table and column names, not row data, except where a question cannot be answered without a sample the product explicitly includes) are sent to our AI provider, currently AWS Bedrock, which processes them to produce the answer. In bring-your-own mode, the same material goes to the provider your administrator configured, under your agreement with that provider. With neither mode configured, questions are processed by a non-generative interpreter inside the service and nothing is sent to an AI provider.

6. Who receives information

We share information only with the providers needed to run the service, each under its own terms:

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, storage, email delivery (SES), and the managed AI provider (Bedrock).
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management.
  • PostHog — product analytics, limited to the counts-and-flags events described above.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the service and its users from abuse or security incidents. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share workspace content with third parties for their own purposes.

7. Email the service sends

The hosted service sends email you configure or trigger: member invitations, scheduled reports, alerts, and digests, delivered via AWS SES. Recipients receive only what your workspace configured for them. Trial workspaces have a low email cap, and sending unsolicited bulk mail through the service is prohibited by our Terms.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Workspace content and account information are kept while the workspace is active.
  • When a workspace is paused for non-payment or trial expiry, data is retained for 30 days so the workspace can be restored, then erased on closure as described in the Terms.
  • Audit-log entries recording that actions occurred are retained after erasure; they contain no workspace content.
  • Technical logs are kept for 90 days and then deleted or anonymized.
  • You may request export or erasure of your workspace's data at any time by contacting us (section 10). Erasure from live systems is prompt; backup copies age out on the normal backup cycle.

9. Security

Access to the hosted service is authenticated and role-based; administrative operator access is restricted and audited. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify workspace owners of a confirmed breach affecting their workspace without undue delay.

10. Your choices and rights

Workspace administrators can manage members and roles in the product, export workspace data using the product's export features, and close the workspace. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us at privacy@getopensight.org and we will respond within the time the applicable law requires.

11. Children and territory

OpenSight is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16; we do not knowingly collect their information. The hosted service is operated from the United States, and information is processed there.

12. Changes to this policy

The current version always lives at getopensight.org/legal/privacy. Material changes will be announced to workspace owners by email at least 14 days before they take effect, with the version and date updated at the top of this page.

13. Contact
