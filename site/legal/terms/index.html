8. AI features

  • Natural-language questions ("Ask O") work in two modes. In managed mode, questions are answered by an AI provider we run (currently AWS Bedrock) and counted against the workspace's monthly AI quota. In bring-your-own mode, a workspace administrator configures the Customer's own AI provider account, and questions are processed by that provider under the Customer's agreement with it.
  • With neither mode configured, Ask O falls back to a non-generative interpreter that runs entirely inside the Service.
  • AI-generated answers can be wrong. The Customer is responsible for reviewing answers before relying on them.
